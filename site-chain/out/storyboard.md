# Storyboard — the Alchemist film

The approved prototype (G1: "TOTALLY APPROVED. IT IS MAGNIFICENT") is the look. The owner picked none of the FURTHER options, so none is planned. The whole film is about 40 viewport-heights of scroll. The space is an illustration of the method, not measured data, and the Bramble iterations are drawn with schematic positions, as the paper's own Figure 16 does.

## Revision after G2 (owner's notes, 17 Nov 2026)

Four sequences are inserted as **holds**. Story time stops while each plays on its own clock, so the beats above keep their choreography and the page grows by 19%. On screen, beats B07, B10 and B13 now also show the stations at work, the fix vector and the judge's estimate.

**Stations at work in the iterations.** Each birth sends a pulse from the compiler (P → C, H → C, C → sₙ). Each judgement brings the verdicts in (T → J) and appends a row (J → H; the obelisk gains a ring). Iteration 4 halts, so the judge is not called.

**The vector-operation question.** The owner asked for the step as an algebraic vector operation. The paper says the new position is not the old one plus the step (C67). The film therefore draws both: the judge's estimate sₙ + fₙ as a ghost, and the real sₙ₊₁ = C(P, f₁…fₙ) at the end of the thread from P.

**Reading.** The page is split into Skill (skill.html: what it does, how a run goes, design rules, when it goes wrong, then the theory in brief) and Paper (paper.html: the paper's own HTML, unaltered apart from an Alchemist navigation pill, with every figure and its interactive layer).

**3D for the paper's figures.** Two figures became 3D sequences: the architecture (B06b) and the step (B10b with B10 and B13). The rest stay on the Paper page, because recreating them in the film would duplicate the reading and crowd the beats.
