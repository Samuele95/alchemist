# Storyboard v2 — the loop, drawn literally

Supersedes the v1 beats after B05. Owner's direction (25 Nov): the website is too cryptic; the cinematic must be coherent with the skill's job; the explanation must be visual. G1b approved the grammar of `loop.html`: the candidate is a scroll of real text, testers are attackers firing recorded attacks, the judge reads damage and never the scroll, the compiler rewrites with visible edits.

## Grammar (every object looks like what it is)

| thing in the skill | on screen |
|---|---|
| candidate sₙ | a scroll: the real recorded prompt, typed in by the compiler; it moves to a new place in the room when rewritten |
| compiler | station ☿ left; glyphs stream from it onto the scroll when it writes |
| tester Tᵢ | station right, with its name; fires its recorded attacks one by one as glyph streams; a feed of the attacks with `held` / `broke` |
| verdict | the scroll ripples (held) or cracks red where the attack lands (broke); the offending line lights up in the text; a pass / fail badge |
| judge | station ♄ below; the scroll goes dark ("unseen by the judge"); verdicts fly from the testers; the diagnosis and the fix are typed at its desk |
| history | the ledger beside the judge gains a row per iteration (verdicts + fix) |
| fix → compiler | the fix flies as a stream; the compiler lights up and rewrites |
| the rewrite | old text dissolves; new text typed with `del` / `ins` from the paper's diff; the scroll moves; a ghost stays at the old place |
| the room | the semantic space from v1 (star s†, faint cloud) as the backdrop: each scroll position is a point; the thread from P joins them |
| coupling | a crack heals where the fix lands and another opens elsewhere, in the same shot |
| convergence | a scroll with no cracks, lit from inside, every attack bouncing off, the room turns gold |
| barrier | the sealed room from v1, now with two scrolls cracking in turn and never both whole |

## Layout rule (fixes the overlaps)

Three fixed columns at 16:9: left 0–30% the compiler and the copy band; centre 30–62% the scroll (DOM, docked, never floating over stations); right 62–100% the tester stations with their feeds in a column under each. The judge's desk is a bottom-centre drawer that opens during the judgement and pushes the copy band up. Labels never move with projection into other columns; they clamp to their column.

## Kept from v1

The veil, the cut, the star, the cloud, the sealed room, the toy, the three tiers, the frame check, the claims. Dropped: the abstract tester planes, the fragments, the diagnosis tubes, the machine beat (B06b, subsumed), the unfold holds (subsumed: the scroll is always the prompt).
